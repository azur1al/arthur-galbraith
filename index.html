≡ About Me ≡

Arthur Galbraith is an experimental author with several succesful novels, poetry collections, and short story collaborations under his belt. Outside of his work as an author, Galbraith is also an editor and publisher who has worked both independently and in collaboration with Honeyeater Publications for the past 37 years.

Due to my daughter finding such great success and travelling far from home for quite a significant amount of time, I have decided to open the callouts for a collaborative short-story project to be edited by myself and published by my good friends at Honeyeater Publishing.

This has been the topic of many ongoing conversations since late 2025, in the midst of writing The Brick House and having it edited by the lovely folk at Honeyeater Publishing. As many of you know, my late wife passed away recently, and it is my hope that this short story collection can be written collaboratively in her honour.

The theme of this collaborative project is loss/freedom.

I am seeking submissions of up to 2,000 words as an expression of interest to participate in this collaborative project.

Do not submit draft works that do not address the theme, are over 2,000, or if you are not able to commit to the project wholly.

The project timeline is as follows...

   EOI submissions close: November 4th, 2026

   Contributors selected: December 2nd, 2026

   For publication in: March, 2027

I am seeking talented authors at all stages in their career, with a focus on uplifting and supporting our upcoming and emerging authors.

To learn more about this opportunity and to submit your EOI, visit this link.

I've been working in the world of writing and publishing since I was of working age, helping my father with his printing press and my mother with her proof reading. One of the first things I learnt as a child was the importance of being honest. Honest with yourself about your limitations. Honest with others about your expectations. An untruthful writer or publisher will only do harm to the industry, to the community, and to those they rely on and are relied upon by.
